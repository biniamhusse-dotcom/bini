Symptoms(DM) (c)
HyperG: Polyuria, Nocturia, Polydipsia, Fatigue.
DKA: Nausea, Vomiting, fast and deep breathing, weakness, worsening Poly symptoms, abdominal pain.
HypoG: Hunger, palpitations, light headedness, dizziness, sweating, extreme fatigue, confusion, bad dreams.
Infections: F: fever, C: cough, SL: skin lesions.
UTI: dysuria, frequency.
DFU: Diabetic Foot Ulcer.
PN: tingling, numbness, burning sensation of Feet and hands.
AutoN: Postural dizziness, Sexual dysfunction.
VisB: Visual Blurring.

Dysp: Dyspnea

LSw: Leg swelling

HA: Headache

NkP: Neck pain and Stiffness
Other: UOP: Decreased Urine Output
B/P (mmHg) (g)
• Measured at every visit with Goal of <140/90 mmHg (check for orthostatic changes when indicated).
Wt(kg), Ht(m), BMI (kg/m²) (e and f)
• Measure height of an adult at enrollment and children every visit.
• Measure weight at initial assessment and at each visit.
• Calculate BMI as: Weight in Kg divided by the square of height in meters (kg/m²).
BMI Interpretations:
<18.5 --- Malnourished | 18.5–24.9 --- Normal
25–29.9 --- Overweight | 30–39.9 --- Obese | >40 --- Morbid Obesity
Risk factors status (d)
Tbco: Tobacco Use including shisha
Phin: Physical inactivity
Alc: Alcohol use (>2 for women and >3 drinks/day for men)
Salt: High Salt consumption
Sug: High Sugar Consumption
Fat: High Fat Consumption
Veg: Low Vegetable and fruit consumption
Khat: Khat Consumption
Cardiovascular (i)
1. Normal Cardiac Exam
2. S3 and/or S4 gallop
3. Murmurs
4. Neck Veins engorged or JVP raised
5. Signs of arrhythmia
Musculoskeletal Exam/ Foot exam (k)
1. Dry skin/Callus/deformity
2. Ulcer
3. Absent/asymmetric pulses, poor capillary filling
4. Reduced or absent light touch, Vibration or position senses
5. Decreased/Absent DTR
6. Infected wound
7. Pedal edema
8. Pretibial edema
9. Normal findings
Oral/Dental (h)
GOH: Adequately hygienic
POH: Poor Oral Hygiene
DNTC: Dental Caries
Ging: Gingivitis
DNTL: Dental Loss
Cand: Oral Candidiasis
Skin and Injection site (j)
1. Normal findings
2. Atrophic injection site
3. Hypertrophic injection site
4. Signs of infection
5. Wrong injection site
Neurologic and Dilated eye exam (l)
• Neurologic: Motor, Sensory and Eye Exam
• Dilated eye exam should be done annually for: all Type 2 patients starting from Dx; all Type 1 annually 5 yrs from Dx; Hypertensive patients with suspected hypertensive urgency or emergency.
Referrals/Consultations (v)
• Refer eligible Diabetic patients to Ophthalmologist for dilated eye exam and document the feedback.
Lab tests (m, n, o, p)
1. FBS (to be done at every visit) or HBA1C every 3 months.
2. U/A: Protein (P-, P+ to P++++) — check annually; Ketone (K-, K+ to K++++) — Type 1 DM every visit and for Type 2 when FBS/RBS >300 mg/dl; Microscopy to see Casts.
3. RFT (Cr): done annually for Type 2 DM; for Type 1 starting 5 years after diagnosis.
Life Style Changes and education (r)
1. Cessation of smoking
2. Exercise (150 min per week)
3. Healthy Diet
4. Reduce alcohol use
5. Medication adherence
6. Medication side effects
7. Daily Foot inspection
8. Family screening
9. Injection Techniques

○ Dead

Adherence (u)
Good: missed <20% of the doses last month
Poor: missed ≥20% of the doses last month
