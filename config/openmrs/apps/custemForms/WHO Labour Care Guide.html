WHO Labour Care Guide: Clinical Guidance for Professionals

The 4-Step Clinical Cycle: Assess → Record → Check → Plan

  • Assess: Evaluate woman's well-being, baby's well-being, supportive care, and labour progress.
  • Record: Document numerical measurements as actual values, and non-numerical findings using standardized abbreviations.
  • Check Alert Threshold: Compare every observation with the "ALERT" reference column. Any value meeting alert criteria is circled in red.
  • Plan: When an alert occurs, alert senior midwife/obstetrician, discuss with the woman/companion, and record the agreed shared management plan.

Key Recommended Practices (WHO Intrapartum Guidelines)

Strongly Recommended (✓)
• Continuous labour companionship of woman's choice.
• Respectful maternity care maintaining dignity and privacy.
• Freedom of mobility and upright birth positions.
• Oral fluid and light nutrition on demand during labour.
• Pharmacological and non-pharmacological pain relief.
• Delayed cord clamping (≥ 1 minute) and skin-to-skin contact.
NOT Recommended / Harmful (✗)
• Routine pubic shaving or enemas on admission.
• Routine continuous CTG for low-risk women.
• Routine amniotomy or routine oxytocin to prevent delay.
• Routine IV fluids in low-risk women (restricts mobility).
• Manual fundal pressure (Kristeller) in second stage.
• Routine episiotomy or oral/nasal suctioning in clear fluid.
